I'm a PhD student in the Kahlert School of Computing at the University of Utah, advised by Jenny Han Lin. My research spans computer graphics, deep learning, and computational fabrication, with a focus on fabrics and textiles, especially knits, from capturing, simulating, and rendering them at the yarn and fiber level to compiling designs for industrial knitting machines. I also build simulation tools for biological imaging, specifically live-cell super-resolution microscopy.

Before coming to Utah, I earned a BS in Applied Mathematics with a specialization in Computing at UCLA, where I did undergraduate research with Sarah Tymochko.

    In preparation for submission to Nature Methods.A collaboration between the OHSU Knight Cancer Institute, HHMI Janelia, and UC Berkeley, continuing work I began as an undergraduate intern.
